# XXD Panel 005 · 童话版画结构叙事

Turn each supplied photograph into finished fairy-tale print artwork. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must compress the same subject into one or two blunt recognisable masses, place it inside one source-grown dark structural field, and guide the story through pale negative space plus a few saturated signal blocks. Flat screenprint, powdery pastel, and sparse imperfect line keep it handmade, weighty, and endearing without becoming a generic children's cartoon.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, action, function, and emotion through at least three source-specific cues while compressing the subject into one or two blunt large masses inside one source-derived dark structural field. It is not tracing, generic cute cartooning, decorative flat shapes, glossy vector art, photoreal painting, 3D, or an unrelated dark frame.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 005 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve identity, action, function, emotion, and at least three defining cues → compress into one or two blunt large masses with slight proportional exaggeration → derive one low-value or high-contrast structural field from the source's space and meaning → make it act as container, path, frame, or density shell through occlusion, cropping, engulfment, and partial reveal → enforce a three-layer palette of dominant dark structure, breathing pale negative space, and tiny saturated story signals → render flat screenprint plus powdery pastel with dry brush, grain, scumble, paper tooth, spray, and slight misregistration → treat any text as a small sign, object panel, or edge note**.

The fairy-tale feeling comes from the source's real identity and the way the dark field hides, carries, frames, or reveals it—not from stock castles, moons, stars, mushrooms, cute faces, or mascots. Picture-book hierarchy, handmade print, editorial illustration, and art publishing are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the blunt silhouette, dark field, occlusion logic, bright signals, or copy. Also reject evenly distributed bright colour, arbitrary black blobs, dense scenery, smooth vector polish, comic outlines, kawaii stickers, photoreal shading, 3D volume, plastic surfaces, and decorative symbols not earned by the source.

## 005 visual system

### One or two blunt recognisable masses

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Compress the principal subject or inseparable relation into one or two clear large masses, with blunt silhouette, slight proportional exaggeration, and only a few decisive edges. Simplification removes detail but preserves identity, action, function, and emotion.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Keep one principal subject or inseparable relationship. Any secondary line, shape, or prop must clarify its gesture, setting, or visual joke.

### Source-grown dark structural field

- Derive one dominant low-value or high-contrast field from the source's space, material, action, or meaning. It must function as a container, path, frame, or density shell rather than a generic dark background.
- Use occlusion, edge cropping, engulfment, and partial reveal to create structural narrative. The shell hides some information so the right identity cues become stronger.
- Organise the picture as a flat storybook page using one suitable device: compressed overhead view, lateral movement, frontal frame, path guidance, scale jump, or edge crop.
- Keep detail low, identity high, and the reading path clear. No dense scene reconstruction or scattered decorative motifs.

### Sparse functional line and handmade print

- Use sparse fine line only for function, route, boundary, texture, and recognition. Keep it handmade and slightly inaccurate without weakening identity.
- Keep every colour mass flat. Combine screenprint density with powdery pastel or crayon, dry brush, grain, scumble, paper tooth, spray specks, broken white, and slight registration shift.
- Material marks follow the hierarchy: the dark field carries weight, pale paper breathes, and saturated signals stay small and crisp enough to guide reading.
- Reject realistic light, complex volume, smooth gradients, polished vector edges, clay, resin, plastic CGI, and studio rendering.

### Fixed three-layer value hierarchy

- Derive hue from the photograph while preserving three stable area/value roles: a dominant dark structural colour for weight and boundary; a moderate pale field or whitespace for breath; tiny high-saturation emotion colours for route and story signals.
- Place bright signals only on the most informative face, pose, path, functional object point, environmental sign, or narrative focus. Dark structure or pale negative shape must keep them visually contained.
- Hue may change with the subject, but dark/pale/signal area ratios and value order remain stable. Never distribute saturation evenly.
- Avoid rainbow palettes, large bright backgrounds, generic black-plus-red drama, muddy mixtures, neon glow, and corporate brand systems.

## Copy and typography belong to 005

005 text behaves like a small sign, object panel, or edge annotation embedded in the print structure—not a headline wall, UI label, data display, or detached overlay.

- Automatic copy derives one concise word or phrase from identity, visible action, function, relation, place when known, or the source-grown structural narrative without inventing a story.
- Prefer one tiny sign-like title plus zero to two edge or object notes. A number, place, or year appears only when supplied or reliably established.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 005 construction:

- **Role:** a low-density sign, object panel, or edge note that helps identify or pace the story.
- **Voice:** rounded, blunt, warm, handmade, restrained, and quietly playful.
- **Construction:** one small rounded title with slightly uneven weight plus at most two smaller notes. Do not force one proportion across scripts.
- **Placement:** attach to a functional object face, dark-field edge, path turn, pale negative region, or canvas margin without covering key cues.
- **Material:** dry ink, crayon, screenprint, or softly imperfect letterpress consistent with the print.
- **Script:** use a native hand-made editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than imitating Latin handwriting across every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

## Raster generation and privacy

Use the host's built-in bitmap image-generation capability by default and follow the available `imagegen` skill when exposed. View each local source before generation. Use one generation call per distinct asset; a wallpaper pack requires four calls. “Hand-drawn”, “flat”, “screenprint”, and “editorial illustration” describe appearance only and never authorise SVG, HTML, CSS, Canvas, diagrams, or programmatic drawing as the artwork.

If built-in bitmap generation is unavailable, use `scripts/configured_imagegen.py`, which reuses an already configured compatible bitmap route and emits sanitised status only:

```bash

  --prompt-file /private/job-temp/transform-prompt.txt \
  --out /private/job-temp/design.png --size 1536x1024 --quality high
```

Judge readiness by actual bitmap capability, not a provider name or one environment variable. Never display, log, persist, or report providers, endpoints, headers, credentials, account identifiers, route configuration, prompts, or response bodies. Do not manually inspect credential files. The bridge's sanitised JSON is the complete allowed diagnostic surface.

An explicit invocation of `/xxd-panel-005` or `$xxd-panel-005` with a source image and requested output authorises generation through any already configured authenticated bitmap route available to the session. Do not ask for a second confirmation solely because the route changes. This does not authorise changing credentials, providers, billing, accounts, or global settings.

Only report generation as unavailable after both built-in capability and the bundled probe fail to establish a usable bitmap route. State the limitation narrowly and never guess the cause or identify the provider. Never substitute code-rendered art.

## Fresh-task and source boundary

Every invocation starts a fresh generation job unless the user explicitly asks to continue, audit, compare, edit, or reuse a named earlier result. Repeating the same source and parameters still means generating a new result in a new task directory. An old file can never satisfy a new request.

Use only images attached to the current invocation, explicit user paths, or a previously supplied image that the current request clearly identifies as “the same image”. Never broadly scan the Desktop, workspace, `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-005/`, or historical task folders to find a substitute source. If the intended source cannot be accessed, ask for it.

      请分别输入手机、iPad、电脑、儿童手表的尺寸

5. Open one fresh job boundary and reserve the next unused sibling output directory for every source and selected mode. Confirm only current user-supplied sources, inspect each separately, and read the full prompt matching the working language:
   - Chinese: [references/xxd-panel-005-prompt.zh-CN.md](references/xxd-panel-005-prompt.zh-CN.md)
   - English: [references/xxd-panel-005-prompt.en.md](references/xxd-panel-005-prompt.en.md)

Privately lock: the principal subject or inseparable relationship; at least three identity, action, function, or emotion cues; the one-or-two-mass silhouette; the source-grown dark field and whether it acts as container, path, frame, or density shell; the occlusion, cropping, engulfment, or partial-reveal plan; one suitable storybook perspective device; the dark/pale/saturated area ratios; the exact locations of tiny saturated signals; the screenprint/pastel texture hierarchy; and any sign, object-panel, or edge-note type placement. 7. Lock one copy package per source and share it verbatim across selected modes by default; honour explicit per-mode overrides. 9. Generate each distinct transformed design alone at the planned aspect and preferably exact frame size. For paired modes, prepare the photographic panel separately. Never ask the model to generate both halves at once.
   Within the same current invocation, ordinary modes may reuse one newly approved transformed intermediate only when frame dimensions, aspect ratio, source facts, aesthetic instructions, typography, and locked copy are identical. Otherwise generate separately. Wallpaper assets are always generated separately.
10. For `independent`, generate four wallpapers directly from the source. For `linked`, generate and visually approve the anchor first; every derivative receives exactly the original source plus that same anchor. The source locks identity and content; the anchor locks only blunt-mass grammar, dark-field role, occlusion logic, three-layer value hierarchy, screenprint/pastel texture, saturated-signal strategy, and typography. 11. Finalise every selected mode in menu order with `scripts/compose_panel.py`, reopen each PNG at normal and thumbnail size, and apply the acceptance gate. Retry only the faulty generated asset once when a hard invariant fails. After one failed correction, return the best result and name the unresolved issue rather than pretending success.
12. Return absolute paths in source order, then menu order 1→4. Wallpaper order is phone, iPad, desktop, watch.

## Composition commands

```bash
# Source-adaptive top-bottom
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout top-bottom --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout top-bottom

# Source-adaptive left-right
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout left-right --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout left-right

# Design-only
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout design-only

# One wallpaper, repeated separately for all four devices
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --design phone.png --out wallpaper-phone.png \
  --layout design-only --size 1440x3200
```

`--size WIDTHxHEIGHT` overrides `--canvas` and source adaptation. Generate the design at the exact planned aspect; if exact pixels are unsupported, use a closest supported same-aspect bitmap and resample proportionally without cropping. Audit with the same layout used to build the result.

## Acceptance gate

Before accepting each result verify:

- Mode, exact pixels, source-adaptive formula, split axis, seam, and output count are correct.
- Visible photography is faithful and type-free; source-hidden outputs contain no source photograph or seam.
- The simplified subject remains recognisable through at least three source-specific cues and preserves the principal action or inseparable relationship.
- The subject is still recognisable through at least three source-specific cues, while the principal subject or inseparable relation is compressed into one or two blunt masses only.
- One source-derived dark field clearly acts as container, path, frame, or density shell. Its occlusion, crop, engulfment, or partial reveal strengthens the story instead of becoming an arbitrary black blob.
- The three-layer hierarchy is unmistakable: dominant low-value structure, breathing pale field, and tiny saturated signals. Bright colour is never spread evenly.
- Saturated signals appear only at informative faces, poses, paths, functional points, environmental signs, or narrative focuses and remain visually contained.
- Flat screenprint and powdery pastel/crayon form a credible hand-made hierarchy with dry brush, grain, scumble, paper tooth, spray, broken white, and slight misregistration—not glossy vector polish, smooth 3D, or generic dark-background cartooning.
- Automatic copy is concise, grounded, and 005-specific; exact user wording remains verbatim. All rendered text is accurate and native to the resolved locale; text-free output contains no text or pseudo-text.
- Typography reads as restrained retro hand lettering or softly irregular print integrated with subject gesture, geometry, or paper space. It never looks like a generic bold poster title, UI label, or detachable digital overlay.
- Every wallpaper is separately recomposed, respects safe regions, contains no system UI, and is not a crop of another device result. A linked pack shares one family without drifting from the source.
- Every delivered PNG was newly generated for this invocation and lives in its fresh task directory.
